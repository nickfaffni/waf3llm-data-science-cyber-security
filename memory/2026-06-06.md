## Key Learnings
- **Scaling helps significantly**: Tuned XGBoost F1 rose from 38.94% to 48.70% (a +9.76% boost) and PR-AUC surged from 32.41% to 53.75% (+21.34%) by scaling benign base pages from 3k to 10k (at 20% ratio).
- **False Alarm reduction**: The false positive rate (FPR) of Tuned XGBoost on hand-crafted natural-benign pages was reduced from 60% down to 20% as a direct result of scaling.
- **RF baseline stability**: Random Forest maintained 0.00% false alarms on the natural-benign sets, and its recall improved to 13.20% on the scaled dataset.
- **Severe imbalance constraints**: At a 10% ratio, the models face a steeper challenge due to the massive minority class ratio, which highlights the need for balanced training sets or threshold sweeps.
