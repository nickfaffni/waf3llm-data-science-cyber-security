Introduction to Cybersecurity Data Science

Published on June 7, 2026 by Nick

Machine learning has revolutionized the way we approach cybersecurity. Traditionally, systems relied heavily on static signatures to detect malware or attacks. However, as attackers became more sophisticated, these signature-based methods fell behind.

Today, we use structural analysis and anomaly detection to identify threats before they execute. One fascinating area is protecting Large Language Models (LLMs) from Indirect Prompt Injections (IPI), where malicious instructions are hidden deep inside the HTML DOM structure of a website.

If you're interested in learning more, check out the research on proactive shift-left defense mechanisms!
